      <div class="dn-intro-text">
        <h1>Dohyun Kim</h1>
        <p>I’m an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, advised by <a href="https://phseo.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Paul Hongsuck Seo</a> in the <a href="https://miil.korea.ac.kr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Multimodal Interactive Intelligence Laboratory (MIIL)</a>.</p>
        <p>My research interests center on multimodal and generative models, with a particular focus on diffusion models. I have worked on topics such as data-efficient compression of diffusion models and preference-based optimization for text-to-image generation. I am broadly open to diverse directions in generative AI, multimodal learning, and beyond.</p>
        <p>I’m currently a Research Intern at Trillion Labs. Previously, I visited the University of Michigan, working with <a href="https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~honglak/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Honglak Lee</a>.</p>
